Privacy Policy

SmanjiVideo — Last updated 2026-11-24

Introduction

SmanjiVideo is developed by Cosmic Production d.o.o., based in Split, Croatia. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Version 1.2.4

Version 1.2.4 includes Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform for advertising. Google may process device identifiers and advertising or usage data for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Where applicable, iOS asks for permission to track your activity across apps and websites. Video compression takes place on your device. See Google’s privacy policy for information about Google’s processing. You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings.

Version 1.2.5

Version 1.2.5 removes the advertising and consent SDKs and does not request tracking permission. Videos you select from Photos or Files are compressed on your device. You choose whether to save a copy to Photos or share it. Apple handles purchases through the App Store. Your compression history and daily export counter are stored on your device. Saving or sharing a video is a separate action you choose.

App Store services

Apple handles distribution and purchases. We can view the reports Apple provides in App Store Connect. Apple’s privacy policy explains how Apple handles its services.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us.

Your Rights

Under GDPR and other applicable laws, you have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data. If you have concerns, contact us.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
