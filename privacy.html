Privacy Policy

Smanji Video
Effective Date: March 7, 2026

Introduction

SoftSplit d.o.o. ("we", "us", or "our"), located in Split, Croatia, built the Smanji Video app. This page informs you of our policies regarding information when you use our app.

No Data Collection

Smanji Video does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information. The app operates entirely on your device.

On-Device Processing

All video compression is performed locally on your device. Your videos never leave your device and are never uploaded to any server. There is no backend, no cloud processing, and no remote server involved.

No Analytics

We do not use any analytics tools, crash reporting services, or tracking technologies. No usage data is collected or transmitted.

No Third-Party Services

Smanji Video does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that could collect your data.

Photos Library Access

The app requests access to your Photos library solely to allow you to select videos for compression and to save compressed videos. This access is used only for these purposes, and your media is never transmitted off your device.

Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any data, there are no special concerns regarding children's privacy. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
