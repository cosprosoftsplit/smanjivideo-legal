Frequently Asked Questions

What does Smanji Video do?

Smanji Video compresses your videos to reduce their file size, making them easier to share and saving storage space on your device.

Is my data safe?

Yes. All video compression is performed entirely on your device. Your videos are never uploaded to any server. The app does not collect any personal data.

Why does the app need access to my Photos?

The app needs access to your Photos library to let you select videos for compression and to save the compressed videos back to your library.

Will I lose quality when compressing?

Video compression involves a trade-off between file size and quality. Smanji Video aims to reduce file size while preserving as much visual quality as possible. You can adjust compression settings to find the right balance.

Can I undo a compression?

The original video remains unchanged in your Photos library. Compressed videos are saved as new files, so your originals are always preserved.
